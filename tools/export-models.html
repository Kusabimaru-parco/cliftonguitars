Export guitars to .glb

Builds every A Series guitar and a few Clifton Customs showcase builds exactly as the site does, and saves each one to models/ as a binary glTF (.glb): metres, Y up, standing on the ground at the origin. Run it with node server.js (the save step needs that local server).

"Render list pictures" saves a picture of every guitar to assets/thumbs/. The guitar list, home lineup and 3D page lineup show these files, so they load without the 3D engine. Re-run it after changing a model.
